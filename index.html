Oral Histories and Stories

The Story of the Banjari Storyteller, Arav

In the wide, dusty plains of Rajasthan, a young Banjara boy named Arav traveled with his family's caravan. Arav loved the journey, but he often wondered, "What is my gift?"

One evening, he heard a storyteller—an old Banjara woman. Arav felt something stir inside him. When he got back to camp, he picked up a small drum and began tapping lightly… Then he added words… Then a rhythm… Then a story.

Later night, his grandmother Nanni placed a silver coin necklace around his neck. "This is to remind you: That our people carry their home inside their hearts and now, you carry our stories."
